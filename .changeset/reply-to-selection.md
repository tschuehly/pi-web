Reply to a selection in the Workbench Chat: select text inside one earlier message and click Reply (or press Escape to dismiss) to stage it as a quote chip in the composer. Quotes from several messages can be staged and removed before sending; they are sent ahead of the reply as Markdown blockquotes anchored to their message (`> [msg <entry id>] excerpt`), and clicking a sent quote scrolls to its source message when it is loaded.
