Durable Chats now get a generated title from their first prompt (a Workstream checkpoint title or `/name` still wins), keep their Pi packages' warnings in the notification inbox, show each running tool in Activity, resolve their Workstream launch token, and show an interrupted answer as interrupted after a host restart. A failed Stop or other failed action in a Workbench Chat now shows in that Chat. Restart the session daemon to enable it.
