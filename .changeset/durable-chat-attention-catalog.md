Durable Chats now mark unread when a turn finishes, sharing the unread catalog with other Chats, take `/name` and `/session`, show context usage, and remind the agent of open question sets with each prompt. Controls a Durable Chat does not support yet (archiving, attachments, shell input, queue promotion and editing, clone and go back, session defaults, enabled-model edits, reload from disk) are hidden or disabled until its host reports them. Restart the session daemon to enable it.
