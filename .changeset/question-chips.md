Show a Chat's open questions as chips above its composer instead of in a Questions side pane. A chip opens a drawer rising from the composer with every open question set, its context beside its questions; Esc closes it. Each Chat tab shows how many questions are open, and grid tiles show their own chips. The Questions header button and pane are gone; Files keeps the side slot.
