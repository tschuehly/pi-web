Durable Chats gain the composer of other Chats: send now, switch, remove and recall queued messages (also after a restart), image and file attachments with transcript images, the per-Chat thinking level, `!command` and `!!command` (output redacted before it is stored), and prompt templates. A Durable host that does not offer one of these blocks its control with a reason. Restart the session daemon to enable it.
